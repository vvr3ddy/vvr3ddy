# Welcome to the Garden

This is a space for *growing* ideas, rather than just publishing them. Unlike a blog, which is chronological, a garden is **topological**.

## Math Rendering

We can render beautiful mathematics using $\LaTeX$.

For example, the solution to $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ is:

$$
x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
$$

## Typography

The design is inspired by paper journals. We use **Merriweather** for the body text to ensure readability, and **Inter** for UI elements to maintain clarity.

> "A garden is a complex of aesthetic and plastic intentions; and the plant is, to a landscape artist, not only a plant - rare, unusual, ordinary or doomed to disappearance - but it is also a color, a shape, a volume or an arabesque in itself." — Roberto Burle Marx
