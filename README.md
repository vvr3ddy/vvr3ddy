### Hello, I'm VVR.

I work at the intersection of **machine learning, wearable sensing, and systems engineering**. My PhD research looks at how we can learn useful representations of human movement from raw wrist accelerometer data, then turn those representations into a better understanding of activity, behaviour, and health.

The question I keep coming back to is simple: **what do we miss when we treat continuous human behaviour as a collection of isolated, fixed-length windows?**

That question has taken me into self-supervised learning, causal sequence models, activity-bout detection, and long-context modelling. I care about the research, but also about the engineering needed to make the ideas reproducible, efficient, and useful beyond a single dataset.

### What I'm working on

- **Learning from unlabelled movement data.** Pretraining wearable time-series models with reconstruction, contrastive learning, and predictive objectives, including JEPA-style approaches.
- **Going beyond window-level activity recognition.** Studying activity bouts, transitions, temporal context, and day-level representations rather than treating every prediction independently.
- **Sequence models for sensor data.** Experimenting with RWKV, Mamba, and other efficient architectures for continuous accelerometry.
- **Bringing sensing closer to the device.** Exploring Wear OS, Android, and lower-level implementation work, with a growing interest in C++, Metal, and efficient ML kernels.

My research uses free-living accelerometer datasets such as **NHANES, Capture-24, and PAAWS**, alongside work on transfer to health cohorts.
